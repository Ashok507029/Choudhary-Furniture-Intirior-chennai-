EXECUTION METHODOLOGY

The Turnkey Execution Journey

A seamless architectural process from laser measurement to handing over the keys.

01

Site Laser Survey

Millimeter-accurate on-site laser mapping of room layouts, angles, and natural lighting channels.

02

3D Architectural Renders

Photorealistic layout models detailing every joint, veneer hue, and lighting cove before production.

03

Material Curation

Touch and inspect real timber samples, Italian marble slabs, and German hardware mechanisms in person.

04

On-Site Master Joinery

Bespoke joinery, wardrobes, and modular framing are handcrafted directly on location by our hereditary Rajasthani artisans for a seamless, zero-gap fit against your walls.

05

Architectural Alignment & Fitting

Our senior craftsmen and lead architects calibrate hinges, Blum soft-close runners, PVD gold hardware, and lighting channels to millimeter precision.

06

100-Point Audit & Handover

Deep-cleaning and strict quality sign-off before handing over your keys with warranty documents.
