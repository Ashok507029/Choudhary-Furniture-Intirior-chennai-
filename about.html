WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Tradition.
Crafted 100% On Site.

Founded in 2001, Choudhary Furniture & Interiors began with a clear purpose: to bridge the gap between architectural vision and actual physical execution.

Most interior firms operate purely as sales consultants who outsource their carpentry to random subcontractors. We took the opposite path: we deploy our own dedicated teams of hereditary Rajasthani master joiners directly to your property.

We do not sell pre-packaged or ready-made catalog furniture. Every wardrobe, kitchen, and wall panel is custom-scribed and handcrafted 100% on-site to match the real physical dimensions, ceiling beams, and architectural contours of your estate.

The Royal CFI Seal of Authenticity

Every piece of furniture and interior joinery crafted on site bears the hallmark of master joinery and a 10-year structural assurance.

ARCHITECTURAL FIDELITY ON LOCATION

The On-Site Master Craftsmanship Model

No standard ready-made catalog boxes with awkward wall fillers. We handcraft directly on your property for a millimeter-perfect flush fit.

Zero-Gap Scribing & Fit

Every cabinet and wardrobe is custom joined against real plaster lines and ceiling heights, eliminating unsightly gaps and filler panels.

Generational Rajasthani Joiners

Ancestral woodcarvers and finish masters trained in traditional joinery, executing flawless veneers, fluting, and fine Italian PU polishes on site.

100% Calibrated Marine Ply

We exclusively use certified BWP Grade 710 calibrated marine plywood. Fully termite-proof and boiling waterproof with 10-year warranty.

Daily Architectural Oversight

Our Lead Project Architect supervises on-site fabrication daily, ensuring blueprint fidelity, laser accuracy, and zero execution flaws.
